TouchGuard

Ghost touch filter

What it does

TouchGuard watches your screen for touches that don't add up — several fingers appearing out of nowhere, or one spot getting stuck — and quietly filters them out before they ever reach your apps. Everything you actually do with your fingers passes through completely untouched.

How to use it

On the Home tab, pick the highest number of fingers you'd ever use at once — most people pick 1 or 2 — and make sure the status at the top says the filter is on. That's the whole job: no daily upkeep, and changing your mind later costs one tap. The Tune tab is only for fixing a specific problem.
