What it does

TouchGuard watches your screen for touches that don't add up — several fingers appearing out of nowhere, or one spot getting stuck — and quietly filters them out before they ever reach your apps. Everything you actually do with your fingers passes through completely untouched.

How to use it

On the Home tab, pick the highest number of fingers you'd ever use at once — most people pick 1 or 2. Make sure the status below it shows the filter running. That's the whole job: no daily upkeep, and changing your mind later costs one tap.
